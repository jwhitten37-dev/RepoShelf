# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RepoShelf a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, personal
attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's
[private security reporting channel](https://github.com/jwhitten37-dev/RepoShelf/security/advisories/new),
marking the report as a code-of-conduct concern. Reports will be reviewed fairly
and kept confidential to the extent possible. Maintainers may remove content,
issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban
participants based on severity and context.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
